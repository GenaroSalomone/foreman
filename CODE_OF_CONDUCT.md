# Code of conduct

foreman is a small project. These rules keep its issues, pull requests and discussions useful to everyone who shows up.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree about the code, the design and the docs, never about the person.
- Be specific and patient. A report with the command, the output and the platform gets fixed; a long thread of opinions does not.
- Assume good faith, and say plainly when you do not understand something.
- Credit other people's work and keep private information (yours and other people's) out of public threads.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing someone's private information, and spam.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove a comment, issue or pull request that breaks these rules, and may block a person who keeps
breaking them. To report a conduct problem privately, use this repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**
(the form is private; say it is a conduct report). Expect an answer within a week.

This applies in every space of the project: the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions.
